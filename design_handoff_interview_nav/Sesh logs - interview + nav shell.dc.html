The lock, in one line

“Locked at midnight. A record you can rewrite isn't a record.”

Shown once, under the reflection, on a locked session — never as a tooltip on a dead button. Before midnight the same slot reads “Editable until midnight”, so the lock is never a surprise. Numbers keep a Correctable tag; the reflection carries Final.
